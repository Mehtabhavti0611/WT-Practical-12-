Vision & Mission

Vision

To be a leading institution in engineering education, research, and innovation, producing globally competent professionals and entrepreneurs.

Mission

  • To provide quality education through innovative teaching-learning methods.
  • To promote research and industry collaboration.
  • To nurture ethical and responsible engineers for a sustainable future.

Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs)

  • PSO1: Apply engineering knowledge to design and develop software and hardware systems.
  • PSO2: Analyze and solve real-world problems using modern tools and technologies.
  • PSO3: Exhibit leadership and teamwork skills with professional ethics.
